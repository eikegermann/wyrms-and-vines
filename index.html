Forest

Living forest: wyrms that get disturbed three times wake up and move. Wyrms that slide players down twice fly away forever. Each vine can be used three times: the first use plants a sprout somewhere else, and when the old vine withers, the sprout shoots up as the new vine.

Iron Wyrm mode (includes the living forest): more wyrms on the board. Vine-cutter ants 🐜 move in and eat vines, and each eaten vine turns into a fortified wyrm with iron plates, one level harder to shake off (for adults: roll a d20, square it, take it away from 600). Hunters 🏹 roam the board: they may beat a wyrm and leave a tunnel 🕳️ that always slides you down, or get eaten and make it fortified. Land on an ant colony to stomp it out. And you must land on 100 exactly: extra steps bounce you back.

Wyrms & Vines

A dice race to square 100 with a little maths along the way. For 1 to 4 players, kids and grown-ups together, and the computer can join in too.

How it works

  • 🎲 Roll and move. Everyone moves with one ordinary die.
  • 🌿 Land on a vine's root? Roll your dice and add them up to grow the vine to your target. Get there and you climb up!
  • 🐲 Land on a wyrm's head? Its grip starts at your target. Roll and take away until it reaches 0, or slide down its back.
  • 🪶 Typing or writing? Each player chooses: type the answers, or write them with the quill, using a finger or a stylus. The quill can show you how to write each number and help you trace it.
  • ✏️ Got a sum wrong? You get one more go at each sum. You can also stop a puzzle at any time and take what comes.
  • 🌳 Living forest (can be switched off): wyrms that are disturbed too often wake up and move, and grumpy ones fly away for good. Vines wear out after three uses, but a new one sprouts somewhere else.
  • ⚙️ Iron Wyrm mode (for the brave): ants eat vines and turn them into armoured wyrms, and hunters fight wyrms and leave tunnels behind. Stomp on ant colonies to stop them. You need an exact roll to land on 100.
  • ⚔️ Land on another player? Challenge them to a read-write-make duel: roll a ten-sided die three times and place each digit as hundreds, tens or ones. The challenged player decides if the highest or lowest number wins. The winner steps forward one square.

Getting started

  1. For each player, pick a colour, type a name and choose a level. Level 1 adds one die up to 15; the Adult level adds big dice up to 200, so grown-ups don't breeze through. Levels 4 and 5 unlock once a player has played a game at level 3.
  2. Add a computer player if you like, set the game speed, and press Start the game!

On an iPad: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen to play fullscreen. Every board has a code (like FERN-42); type it in to play the same board again.
