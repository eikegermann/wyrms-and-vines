# 7. Vine and wyrm puzzles: add up or take away to a level's target

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** November 2026 (recorded 2026-11-22)

## Context

The game practises the addition duels from Dragon & Bard. Children and adults should be able to play together, with the adults properly challenged. Movement must stay fair across levels.

## Decision

- **Moving:** everyone moves with one d6. Levels only affect the puzzles.
- **Levels:** Level 1 rolls 1d6 to 15; Level 2, 2d6 to 20; Level 3, 1d8 to 25; Level 4, 2d8 to 35; Level 5, 1d20 to 60; Adult, 1d100 to 200.
- **Vine (heat):** start at 0 and keep rolling and adding until you reach the target, then climb.
- **Wyrm (cold):** start at the target and keep rolling and taking away until you reach 0, or slide down. A roll at least as big as what's left clears the wyrm without a sum, so children never meet negative numbers.
- **No roll limit.** A hard limit was considered and rejected: one d6 reaches 15 in three rolls only about 1 time in 11, which would make it mostly luck.
- **One retry per sum.** A second wrong answer on the same sum fails the puzzle.
- **Computer players** get about 88% of sums right.

## Consequences

Puzzles are about the maths, not luck. Level 3 (1d8 to 25) takes the most sums, about 5–6, so it may need tuning. A puzzle's length varies with the rolls.
